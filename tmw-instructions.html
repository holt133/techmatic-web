User Instructions

The app consists of Game Packs where each pack contains word puzzles with 3 words. The objective of the game is to find the fourth word which is related, or linked to the three. For example:

CHESTNUTS * DINNER * POTATOES* (5)

The answer is ROAST and the number next to it indicates the number of letters required. If you type the wrong word 3 times, the app then gives the hint which letter the word starts with. Once you solve all the puzzles in a pack, a Victory Badge pops up. You do not need to complete a Pack to play the next one. Play any Pack at any time regardless of where you are with the current one.

The app has two types of Game Packs:

  1. Themed, e.g. FOOD, NATURE, SCIENCE, MUSIC.
    Click on any of them to start playing immediately. These packs are fairly easy and designed to train the brain without too much strain.
  2. Prize Packs where you have a chance of winning £100 upon solving all the puzzles in a pack. These packs are more difficult to play, but very rewarding for the satisfaction of finding the correct words. The objective is the same for all the puzzles: Find the missing word.
IMPORTANT!

Please read our Terms and Conditions and the Privacy Policy. You can read them on our website: https://techmatic.ai/the-missing-word.html but you must tick the boxes to confirm that you have read them in the app.

To play the Prize Packs:

  1. Click on one of them and register with your email address and password.
  2. The app will prompt you to enter your Date of Birth to confirm that you are 18 years old or over. This is because a monetary reward is involved in the game.
  3. Navigate to Terms & Conditions (T&C) and Privacy Policy and tick the confirmation boxes.
  4. Return to the main screen and start playing.
Enjoy the game.
